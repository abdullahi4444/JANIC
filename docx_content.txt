3. About JANIC
Where Education Meets Innovation
Jazeera Nexus Innovation Center (JANIC), formerly Jazeera Innovation Technology (JIT), was founded on October 25, 2021, by the Faculty of Computer Science & IT at Jazeera University in Mogadishu, Somalia.
JANIC was established to bridge the gap between academic theory, technological innovation, and real-world market needs. Its work combines software development, process automation, practical ICT training, applied research, startup incubation, and technology-focused community engagement.
4. Vision

8. Student Innovation Showcase
From Classroom Ideas to Real-World Solutions
Project 01 — MAAL HUB
Investment & Entrepreneurship Platform
A platform connecting investors with small-business owners and individuals with innovative business ideas.
Category: Web Platform
Project 02 — SAHAL SACCO
Brand Identity & Digital Design
A complete branding project focused on trust, financial accessibility, community development and long-term growth.
Category: Branding & Graphic Design
Project 03 — BADBAADO
Healthcare Coordination Platform
A lightweight, secure, API-first healthcare coordination platform supporting inter-hospital patient handoffs and emergency triage alerts.
Category: HealthTech / API
Project 04 — ROBOT CAR CLEANER
Autonomous Waste Collection Robot
An Arduino-based robotic vehicle designed to detect obstacles, navigate and collect small pieces of waste.
Category: Robotics / Arduino
Project 05 — STUDENT FILE
Duplicate File Detection & Storage Management
A system designed to identify duplicate student files, reduce storage usage and improve file management.
Category: Information Systems
Project 06 — JANIC DOCUMENTARY
Documenting the JANIC Innovation Journey
A video-editing and documentary project documenting the JANIC program, activities, people and innovation journey.
Category: Media & Digital Storytelling
Project 07 — SMART DUST PIN
Smart Dry & Wet Waste Separation
An Arduino-based smart waste management system designed to detect and separate dry and wet waste.
Category: IoT / Arduino
Project 08 — AUTO WATER
Contactless Automatic Water Dispenser
An Arduino-based automatic dispenser that detects a hand or container and controls water flow without physical contact.
Category: IoT / Automation
